# Daily content and archive

- The archive offers today plus the previous 30 calendar days.
- Date-based generators supply future daily puzzles without a server job.
- Letterlight cycles through `WORD_ANSWERS` in `dist/daily-content.js`: 424 everyday, cosy 5-letter words, shuffled once. No word repeats within 365 days at one difficulty, and the levels sit 141 days apart in the cycle. Answers must be words a fluent non-native reader knows. Skip rare or archaic words, plurals, sad or violent words, and rhyme traps like -IGHT/-OUND. Avoid Q/X/Z/J unless the word is very familiar. `verify-words.cjs` checks all of this with a denylist. Puzzles that are already started keep their word: the old 96-word cycle uses `contentVersion:2` without `words:3`, and the older random pick has no `contentVersion`. Dropped answers stay valid guesses.
- Reasoning uses eight templates with date-specific values, hints, and worked answers. Templates recur; the questions vary.
- Sudoku, dominoes, and Little Neighbours are generated from the date and difficulty. Bloom Trail rotates through full paths with distinct clue layouts, without repeats within 61 days at each difficulty.
- Time Lens (shown only on days before the 2026-11-21 cutover, below) uses 183 active local photos in a fixed 61-day cycle, three per day: one history-shaping photo (`iconic: true`, for example the first flight, the Solvay Conference, Earthrise, independence in 1947, Kargil, Chandrayaan-3), one Indian-context photo and one more scene, shown in a shuffled order. No image repeats within any 61 consecutive dates for newly started rounds. The same date uses the same photos across difficulty levels; the clues change.
- Time Lens has no points or score. Each photo allows 3 tries (`TL_TRIES`), and a guess within `TL_WINDOW` (5/3/2 years for Gentle/Medium/Tricky) is found. Misses nudge earlier or later (within 10, within 25, more than 25 years). Finding 2 of 3 (`TL_NEED`) completes the card; otherwise the round is saved as `{missed:1, revealed:1}` and never counts. Saves from before tries stored only `{guess}`, and they are judged by the same window. Each reveal also shows how many years away the guess was. Time Lens hints never give a year. Every active photo has a curated `clue` (required by `verify-window.cjs`), shown at every level. Easy adds a "world back then" line for the photo's decade (`ERA_DECADE` in `dist/extras.js`), Medium adds a broader generation line (`ERA_GENERATION`, 25-year bands), and Hard adds nothing. `verify-window.cjs` checks that these lines cover every active photo and contain no digits.
- Photos judged inappropriate are deleted outright (entry and image) and listed in `REMOVED_PHOTOS` in `dist/extras.js` with a same-year active stand-in, so saves that name them still load with the same answer. Removed so far: "Chema women (1930)" (Basel Mission ethnographic portrait), replaced by C. V. Raman's Nobel-year photo (1930, also in its legacy slot); the Copley Square streetcars (1920) were brought back from retirement to keep 183 active photos.
- The photo cycle repeats after 61 days. It is not an indefinitely growing archive.
- Started puzzles preserve their old words, reasoning, and saved photo IDs. Those legacy photo sets may repeat images from the new schedule. Weaker entries are marked `retired: true` rather than deleted, so saved IDs still resolve; retired photos never enter new rounds.
- Source catalogue dates, creator credits, and applicable licence links appear after guessing. Historical catalogue dates are not independently authenticated dates.
- Progress stays in browser-local storage. It is not shared between devices.

## Feed games

Like the original games, the nine feed games are built from the date and the difficulty level, so past days look the same every time you open them.

- **Story Shuffle** draws from the 61 stories in `dist/stories.js`. Dates before 2026-11-13 (`STORY_V2_FROM`) keep the original 22-story cycle. From that date, each level shows the 39 newer stories first and then the original 22, so no story repeats within 61 days and two levels never share a story on the same day. Keep the first 61 stories in place and in order. Both cycles have fixed sizes (`STORY_V1_COUNT`, `STORY_V2_COUNT`). To add stories, append them and start a new dated cycle from a future date, so days already shown never change.
  - There are no clue lines. The order has to come from the story text itself, so each beat needs natural time and cause cues ("That evening…", "Once it's baked…").
  - Each story has an `accepted` list of orders. The canonical order comes first. Add other orders only when two middle beats really could happen either way round (for example Paper boat, Garden swing and Moving in).
  - Any accepted order solves the puzzle.
  - Tricky hides the card titles, so the cues must still work from the body text alone.
  - On Gentle and Medium, "Check order" says how many cards are in a sensible place. On Tricky it says how many opening cards read right.
  - The hint moves one card into its correct place.
- **Letterlight Ladder** uses `dist/ladders.js`, which holds 90 shortest-route ladders per level. Every step must be an everyday word.
- **Colour Alchemy** works through a shuffled cycle of every recipe at the day's level:

  | Level | Drops | Recipes |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Gentle | 2–4 | 15 |
  | Medium | 3–6 | 35 |
  | Tricky | 4–7 | 124 |

- **Mirror Garden**, **Lantern Lanes**, **Garden Beds**, **Sudoku Six**, **Sudoku Six · Hard**, **Killer Six**, **Bloom Trail 5×5** and **Neighbours Goal** are generated from scratch each day.
- **Mirror Garden** (`mirrorModel`) grows to 5×5 / 6×6 / 7×7 with 3 / 4 / 5 mirrors and 1 / 3 / 5 stones. Every garden has exactly one way to light all the flowers (checked by `mirrorSolve`), and of several unique candidates the day keeps the one the solver works hardest on. Average solver nodes over 2026 went from 18 / 42 / 104 to about 49 / 153 / 580; before, only 311 and 300 of 366 Medium and Tricky gardens were unique. Gardens already started on the old generator keep it (`savedMirror`, `legacyMirrorModel`).
- **Lantern Lanes** (`lanternModel`, dist/lanterns.js) drops random walls, then lights the grid with bulbs. Gentle (5×5, about 6 walls, no numbers) sets the budget to the fewest bulbs that can light everything, and that layout must be the only one. Medium (6×6) and Tricky (7×7) number the walls from the answer, then hide numbers while `lanternSolve` still finds exactly one solution. Medium keeps at least 2 numbers and the bulb budget; Tricky keeps at least 1 and has no budget. Gentle keeps the easiest of 3 candidates, Medium the hardest of 4, and Tricky the hardest of 8. Average solver nodes over 2026: 49 / 125 / 238. Average numbers shown: 0 / 4.4 / 6.9.
- **Garden Beds** (`bedsModel`, dist/beds.js) fills the grid from the top-left with random rectangles (Gentle 5×5 with beds of 2–4 squares, Medium 6×6 with 2–6, Tricky 7×7 with 2–9 and a lean towards bigger beds), then drops one seed packet in each. `bedsSolve` must find exactly one way to cut the garden. Gentle keeps the easiest of 3 candidates; Medium the hardest of 10 and Tricky the hardest of 24, with decoy rectangles breaking ties. Over 2026: average solver nodes 9.3 / 16.1 / 18.2, decoy rectangles per packet 1.24 / 1.77 / 2.58, and the share of gardens that need a guess rather than square-by-square deduction 0% / 57% / 83%.
- **Killer Six on Tricky** uses its own generator (`hardKillerModel`): cages of 3–4 cells (never a single cell), a unique solution, and naked singles alone never finish it. Since 2026-11-09 (v2, saved as `hard: 2`) it adds a few givens, chosen from the cells hidden singles can't reach, until hidden singles with cage sums always finish it, so it never needs guessing. Over 2026 it averages about 3.5 givens (at most 5) and 10 cages; the first version (`hard: 1`, about 1.6 givens, only 54 of 366 days solvable without guessing) still loads for games started on it. Gentle and Medium keep the original generator; Gentle starts from 12 givens and keeps adding until naked singles alone can finish it (about 13 givens), Medium averages about 8.6. Since 2026-11-12 (v3, `softKillerModel`, saved as `hard: 3`) new Tricky games start from the v2 grid plus one more given: the open cell that lets naked singles fill the most other cells. That averages about 4.5 givens (at most 6); naked singles alone finish about 6 in 10 grids, and hidden singles always do. Games saved as `hard: 2` keep their v2 puzzle.
- **Sudoku Six · Hard** (Tricky only, `hardSixModel`) strips clues to a minimal unique grid (at most 11, about 9.7 on average, against 11 for Sudoku Six on Tricky before) and keeps only grids that naked singles alone can't finish; about 1 in 9 days also needs more than hidden singles. Tricky Killers started before this change keep their original puzzle (`savedKiller`).
- **Time Lens Duel**: only the middle photo's own decade solves it (`duelTry`). Misses are kept in `wrongDecades` and point earlier or later. Duels finished before this with a wrong decade keep their solve.
- **Where in the World** (`dist/globe.js`, game 24) uses `PLACES` in `dist/places.js`, generated by `tools/build-places.py` from `tools/places-meta.json` (downloaded by `tools/fetch-places.py`: free-licence Commons photos only, 800 px) and the hand-written `tools/places-text.json` (title, alt, clue, story). There are 277 photos: 90 Gentle, 99 Medium and 88 Tricky (94 at launch, 30 more on 2026-11-12, and 153 more on 2026-11-21 for the v2 cycle, with 9–10 Indian photos per level and more from Africa, South America, Central Asia and Oceania). dist/places/ is about 26 MB (11 MB before the v2 additions). Two very wide panoramas, `g-alhambra` and `g-victoria`, were cropped to 2:1 from a larger Commons size so they still read inside the 300 px cover frame, and `m-varanasi` was cropped to lose a sign naming its ghat; every other photo is the plain 800 px resize. Days before `PLACE_V2_FROM` (`2026-11-22` in `dist/globe.js`, the day after the pool deploy) keep the v1 cycle, `cycleIndex("place-v1-" + level)` over the original 124 photos, which `PLACE_V1` pins by id in their original order; verify-places.cjs checks a fingerprint of every 2026 v1 pick. From `PLACE_V2_FROM` each level cycles its whole pool (`"place-v2-"`), up to 31 newer photos first so the switch repeats nothing from the last month, and no photo repeats within 31 days. To add photos later, start a v3 cycle from a future date rather than growing v2 in place. Gentle photos are world-famous landmarks, Medium recognisable cities, old towns and natural wonders, Tricky everyday street scenes with signs, people and landscape. Alt text and clues never name the country, and Tricky alt text and clues never name the city. Every Tricky entry has a `city` (its common English name, or null for nature and rural scenes; 69 cities, 19 null). From `PLACE_V2_FROM`, Tricky asks for the city whenever the photo has one (on Tricky the clues are in the streets); null-city photos keep the country question. The four cities come from `WORLD_CITIES` in `dist/globe.js`, a hand-written table of every Tricky photo's city plus up to seven more real cities per country, each city listed once: one or two more from the photo's country (when it has others), then one city each from neighbouring countries in the same `WORLD_REGIONS` region, then the rest of the continent (seed `place-city`). Hint 1 names the country instead of the continent, or the region for a city-state like Singapore, where the country would give the answer away. A city question saves the answer as `city`; saves without it stay country questions. Over the v2 cycle about 25 of the first 31 Tricky days ask for a city. Adding or reordering cities in `WORLD_CITIES` changes v2 Tricky options, so do it only before `PLACE_V2_FROM` or with a new cycle; every new Tricky city must be added to it (verify-places.cjs checks). No id, Commons file, image or place may appear twice (`PLACE_ALIASES` in verify-places.cjs maps other names for a place already in the pool), and CC photos must credit their author. Decoy countries come from `WORLD_REGIONS`; adding a country there changes v1 options, so verify-places.cjs would fail. A started game pins `placeId` and `options`.
- **Time Lens Duel** reuses the active Time Lens photos in `dist/photos.js`, always includes at least one history-shaping photo and never uses today's Time Lens photos. A started Duel pins its photo IDs; Duels saved before pinning use the original 183-photo model (`legacyDuelModel`).
- Shiny cards are picked with `rng(`${date}|shiny|${game}`)() < 0.05`, so the same cards are shiny every time you load a day. Changing that formula changes which past cards are shiny.
- The feed counts 20 cards for each day and level from 2026-11-10 (19 on earlier days, which predate Where in the World): 15 games (Lantern Lanes, Garden Beds and Where in the World on every level, and one per family: Pocket Sudoku / Sudoku Six / Sudoku Six · Hard, Killer Six on all three, Bloom Trail / 5×5 / 5×5, Little Neighbours / Neighbours Goal / Neighbours Goal) plus five Aha track cards in one block, generated in `dist/aha.js`. The original five games keep their own progress records, so older saves still work.
- **New-games cutover, 2026-11-21 (`NEW_GAMES_FROM` in `dist/daily-content.js`).** From that day, Last Petal (25) takes Time Lens's slot (5), Code Garden (26) takes Time Lens Duel's slot (15), and unplayed Aha blocks use rotation 3 (`AHA_RING_V3`, with Who Knows?); the day stays at 20 cards. Days before it keep Time Lens, Time Lens Duel and their rotation-2 Aha categories exactly as they were, and solves on them keep counting. Today's feed switched over with the deploy, so anyone who had already played Time Lens that day keeps the save but it no longer counts towards the 20.
- **Later cleanup, on or after 2026-12-22.** By then the 31-day archive (today plus 30 days back) holds no day before the cutover: 2026-11-20, the last day with Time Lens and Time Lens Duel, leaves it after 2026-12-20, so 2026-12-22 leaves a day of margin. A follow-up can then remove their renderers, `PHOTO_BANK` (`dist/photos.js`) and `dist/photos/`, keeping index 5 and 15 saves loading harmlessly and never counting. Indices 5 and 15 are never reused. Until then, keep all of it.

Run `node verify-window.cjs`, `node verify.cjs`, `node verify-extras.cjs` and `node verify-sparks.cjs` before changing the generators or the content banks.

## Photographs

`dist/photos.js` is the canonical image manifest. Every referenced image is bundled under `dist/photos/`; gameplay does not depend on Wikimedia or NASA image servers being available. Commons previews may be resized/re-encoded for delivery; no substantive image changes are intended. Original source pages and licence links remain attached to each entry.

Entry fields: `image`, `year`, `title`, `alt`, `story`, `credit`, `source`, `licenseUrl`, `sourceDate`, `india`, plus `clue` (required for active photos; shown before the guess and must not contain a year), optional `iconic`, `why` (one-line "why it mattered", shown after the guess) and `retired`. Keep the first 183 entries in their original order (legacy schedules depend on it) and append new photos. Only freely licensed images (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, GODL-India, Flickr Commons no known restrictions) are allowed; press-agency photos (AP, Reuters, Getty, etc.) and fair-use files are not. `tools/fetch-historic.py` checks the Commons licence, then downloads and resizes an image (800 px, JPEG q80) and prints its credit metadata.

Do not overwrite the manifest with the old six-photo starter script. `python3 tools/check-photos.py` verifies the full local collection instead (needs Pillow).
